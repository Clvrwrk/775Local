# Local775 future directory pricing page research

**Decision:** Prepare a future-facing pricing page without changing current listing, publication, ranking, billing, or entitlement behavior.  
**Geography:** Reno/Sparks and the wider northern Nevada 775 service area.  
**Research date:** 2026-08-25.  
**Status:** Decision support and page copy, not a commercial launch approval.

## Method and source hierarchy

The review prioritized current first-party product and policy sources from Google, Yelp, BBB, and the FTC. Competitors were included when they offer a local-business profile or directory page and visibly separate a useful free presence from paid presentation, trust, or response tools. Vendor survey claims are treated as directional marketing evidence, not independent market truth.

## Observed facts

- Google Business Profile is available at no charge and supports business hours, website, phone, location or service area, photos/videos, and customer reviews. A paid Local775 plan therefore cannot justify itself merely by withholding accurate directory basics. [Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811?hl=en-en)
- Yelp’s current Page Upgrades emphasize controlled photo ordering, business highlights, a logo, posts, and a call-to-action. Yelp says the bundle averages $6 per day and cites a vendor-run comparison result; that conversion claim is a marketing signal, not an independent benchmark for Local775. [Yelp for Business](https://business.yelp.com/products/upgrades/)
- BBB publicly distinguishes a free profile from an enhanced accredited profile. Its enhanced profile includes a logo, photos/video, products/services descriptions, and Get a Quote. Accreditation has separate eligibility standards and annual dues rather than being treated as a purchased verification badge. [BBB profile comparison](https://www.bbb.org/local/1296/accreditation-benefits/bbb-profile), [BBB Accreditation Standards](https://www.bbb.org/business/accreditation/standards)
- The FTC’s Reviews and Testimonials Rule took effect October 21, 2024. It addresses fake or false reviews, sentiment-conditioned incentives, undisclosed insider reviews, review suppression, and misrepresented independent review sites. Paid plans must not sell favorable reviews or imply that payment produces independent trust. [FTC Q&A](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers)

## Proxies and limitations

- Google, Yelp, and BBB are national products, so their feature patterns are proxies for what northern Nevada businesses already recognize. They do not establish Local775 willingness to pay.
- No primary source found a directly comparable Reno/Sparks directory publishing the same four-plan price ladder. The approved Local775 prices are owner decisions, not market-derived estimates.
- The Premium monthly price is missing by design. The page states the annual formula—ten monthly payments for twelve months—without inventing a dollar amount.
- No checkout, billing terms, refund policy, plan-change policy, or entitlement automation was researched or approved in this slice.

## Recommendations traced to evidence

1. Keep Free useful: accurate contact facts, hours, service area, owner corrections, and a modest visual profile. This matches the baseline users already receive from Google and avoids coercive data quality.
2. Differentiate paid plans with presentation depth, media, offers, inquiry tools, and reporting. This follows the visible product separation on Yelp and BBB.
3. Keep Claim authority, verification, organic ranking, and sponsored placement distinct from payment. BBB’s eligibility separation and FTC disclosure rules reinforce the existing Local775 boundary.
4. Show exact monthly and annual amounts together. Basic is $10/month or $120/year; Standard is $15/month or $180/year. Neither currently claims an annual discount.
5. Describe Premium annual billing as “pay for 10 months, receive 12” until the monthly rate is approved. Derive the eventual annual dollar amount from one configuration value.
6. Mark the page as a future preview, keep it out of navigation, disable paid calls to action, and apply `noindex, nofollow` until commercial launch review is complete.

## AEO fan-out questions — hypotheses to validate

These are question hypotheses for future Search Console, site-search, interviews, and SERP validation; they are not observed demand volumes.

1. How much does it cost to list my business in a Reno directory?
2. Is a Local775 business listing really free?
3. What is included in the free Local775 listing?
4. What is the difference between Free, Basic, Standard, and Premium?
5. Can I pay for a Local775 listing monthly?
6. Is there a discount for paying annually?
7. How are the two free Premium months calculated?
8. Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
9. What happens to my listing if I cancel a paid plan?
10. Does paying improve my organic directory ranking?
11. Are paid businesses labeled as Sponsored?
12. Does a paid plan verify that I own the business?
13. Does Local775 verify licenses or insurance?
14. How many photos can I add on each plan?
15. Can customers request a quote from my listing?
16. Can I publish an offer or coupon?
17. Does Local775 provide a 775 tracking phone number?
18. What reporting is included with each plan?
19. Do I need a separate plan for each business location?
20. When will paid Local775 plans be available?

## Approved pricing contract for the preview

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Preview behavior |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Free Claim CTA may link to the existing Claim flow. |
| Basic | $10 | $120 | Visible for comparison; purchase disabled. |
| Standard | $15 | $180 | Visible for comparison; purchase disabled. |
| Premium | Not set | 10 × monthly rate | Visible for comparison; purchase disabled; annual copy says two months free. |

The plan feature bullets are future product copy only. They do not create current entitlements or supersede the product specification.
